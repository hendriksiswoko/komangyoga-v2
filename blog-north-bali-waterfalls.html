North Bali

Best Waterfalls in North Bali

MyBroBali Travel Guide · 4 min read

North Bali is home to some of the island's most breathtaking waterfalls, offering visitors a peaceful escape from the busy tourist areas of southern Bali. Surrounded by lush tropical forests, cool mountain air, and scenic landscapes, this region is a paradise for nature lovers and adventure seekers alike.

One of the highlights is Sekumpul Waterfall, widely regarded as Bali's most beautiful waterfall. Hidden within a lush valley, its multiple cascading streams create a spectacular natural setting that rewards visitors after a short trek through the jungle.

Another must-visit destination is Banyumala Twin Waterfalls, famous for its crystal-clear natural pool and tranquil atmosphere. It's the perfect place to relax, take stunning photos, or enjoy a refreshing swim surrounded by untouched nature.

Your journey through North Bali is also complemented by stops at the iconic Handara Gate, one of Bali's most photographed landmarks, and the beautiful Ulun Danu Beratan Temple, a lakeside temple that appears to float on the calm waters of Lake Beratan.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to explore North Bali is during the dry season (April to October) when the weather is ideal for hiking and sightseeing. Since North Bali is located around 2.5 to 3.5 hours from Bali's main tourist areas, an early departure is recommended to maximize your time at each destination.

Travel Tips

To ensure a comfortable trip, wear comfortable walking shoes, bring a light jacket, sunscreen, a swimsuit if you plan to swim, and a camera to capture the incredible scenery. Some waterfall trails involve stairs and uneven paths, so a moderate level of fitness is recommended.

Why Visit North Bali?

Unlike the crowded beaches of South Bali, North Bali offers a slower pace, cooler temperatures, and some of the island's most spectacular natural attractions. Whether you're hiking to hidden waterfalls, exploring scenic mountain landscapes, or visiting one of Bali's most iconic temples, North Bali promises an unforgettable adventure filled with stunning views and authentic island charm.
