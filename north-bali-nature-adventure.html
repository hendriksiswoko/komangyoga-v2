About this tour

Escape the crowds and discover the peaceful beauty of North Bali with our North Bali Nature Adventure. This full-day private tour takes you through Bali's breathtaking mountain landscapes, lush tropical forests, majestic waterfalls, and one of the island's most iconic lakeside temples.

Your journey begins at the famous Handara Gate, one of Bali's most photographed landmarks. Surrounded by misty mountains and lush greenery, this iconic Balinese gate provides the perfect backdrop for unforgettable photos.

Continue your adventure to the spectacular Sekumpul Waterfall, widely regarded as Bali's most beautiful waterfall. Hidden deep within a tropical valley, this breathtaking natural wonder rewards visitors with cascading waterfalls, vibrant rainforest scenery, and a refreshing atmosphere. Enjoy a scenic trek through local villages, rivers, and lush plantations before reaching the falls.

Next, visit the serene Banyumala Twin Waterfall, a hidden paradise nestled within Bali's highlands. Surrounded by dense jungle and crystal-clear natural pools, this peaceful destination is perfect for relaxing, swimming, or simply enjoying the beauty of nature.

Complete your journey at the iconic Ulun Danu Beratan Temple, one of Bali's most photographed landmarks. Situated on the shores of Lake Beratan, this stunning floating temple appears to rise gracefully from the water, creating one of the island's most picturesque and spiritual landscapes.

Whether you're an adventure enthusiast, a photography lover, or simply looking to experience Bali's natural beauty beyond the popular tourist areas, the North Bali Nature Adventure offers an unforgettable escape into the island's cooler highlands.

Good to Know

  • This tour involves moderate trekking, especially at Sekumpul Waterfall.
  • A reasonable level of fitness is recommended.
  • Swimming at Banyumala Twin Waterfall is optional and weather permitting.
  • Paths to the waterfalls may be slippery, so proper footwear is recommended.
  • The weather in North Bali is generally cooler than in southern Bali, so bringing a light jacket is advisable.
  • The itinerary may be adjusted depending on weather and traffic conditions.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How difficult is the trek to Sekumpul Waterfall?+

The trek is considered moderate and includes stairs, uneven paths, and river crossings. It is suitable for most travellers with a reasonable level of fitness.

Can I swim at the waterfalls?+

Yes. Swimming is possible at Banyumala Twin Waterfall and may also be possible at Sekumpul Waterfall depending on weather and safety conditions.

Are entrance fees included?+

Yes. All entrance fees are included in the all-inclusive package.

Is this tour suitable for children?+

Yes, although children should be comfortable with walking and moderate trekking. Adult supervision is recommended throughout the tour.

What happens if it rains?+

The tour operates in most weather conditions. However, if heavy rain makes trekking unsafe, the itinerary may be adjusted to ensure the safety and comfort of all guests.
