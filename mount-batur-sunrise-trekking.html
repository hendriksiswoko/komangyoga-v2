About this tour

Experience the magic of Bali from above with the Mount Batur Sunrise Trekking tour, one of the island's most iconic outdoor adventures. Perfect for nature lovers and hiking enthusiasts, this unforgettable journey rewards you with breathtaking sunrise views from the summit of one of Bali's active volcanoes.

Your adventure begins in the early morning with a guided trek to the summit of Mount Batur. As you hike under the stars with the guidance of an experienced local trekking guide, you'll witness the first rays of sunlight illuminate the surrounding mountains, Lake Batur, and the distant silhouette of Mount Agung.

Upon reaching the summit, enjoy a delicious breakfast while overlooking one of Bali's most spectacular panoramic views. The peaceful atmosphere and unforgettable scenery make this a once-in-a-lifetime experience and a favourite among travellers visiting Bali.

After descending the mountain, relax your muscles at the Batur Natural Hot Spring, where you can soak in warm mineral-rich pools while enjoying stunning views of Lake Batur. Before returning to your hotel, visit a traditional Bali Coffee Plantation to discover the island's coffee-making traditions and enjoy complimentary tastings of locally produced coffee and herbal teas.

Whether you're an experienced hiker or a first-time adventurer, the Mount Batur Sunrise Trekking tour offers the perfect combination of adventure, relaxation, and natural beauty for an unforgettable Bali experience.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How difficult is the Mount Batur trek?+

The trek is considered moderate and is suitable for beginners with a reasonable level of fitness. An experienced local guide will accompany you throughout the hike.

Is breakfast included?+

Yes. A light breakfast is served at the summit while you enjoy the sunrise.

Can children join this tour?+

Yes. Children with good physical fitness can participate when accompanied by an adult. We generally recommend this tour for children aged 10 years and above.

Do I need hiking equipment?+

No special equipment is required. We recommend wearing comfortable hiking shoes and warm clothing. A flashlight or headlamp will be provided.

What happens if it rains?+

The trek usually operates in light rain. However, if weather conditions are considered unsafe, the itinerary may be adjusted or rescheduled to ensure the safety of all participants.
