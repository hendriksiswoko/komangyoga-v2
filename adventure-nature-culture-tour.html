ATV Quad Bike, Monkey Forest & Batuan Temple Tour

Experience the perfect combination of adventure, nature, wildlife, and Balinese culture with our ATV Quad Bike, Monkey Forest & Batuan Temple Tour. This exciting full-day private tour takes you off the beaten path, allowing you to explore Bali's lush countryside, encounter playful monkeys in their natural habitat, and visit one of the island's oldest and most beautiful temples.

Your adventure begins with an exhilarating ATV Quad Bike experience through Bali's breathtaking tropical landscapes. Ride across muddy tracks, dense bamboo forests, rivers, rice fields, jungle trails, tunnels, and natural obstacles designed to provide an unforgettable off-road adventure. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced rider, professional instructors will provide a full safety briefing before your ride, ensuring an exciting and safe experience for everyone.

After working up an appetite, enjoy a delicious lunch overlooking the beautiful Balinese countryside before continuing your journey to the cultural heart of Ubud.

Your next destination is the famous Ubud Monkey Forest, a lush nature reserve and sacred sanctuary home to hundreds of playful long-tailed macaques. Wander beneath towering tropical trees, explore ancient moss-covered temples, and experience one of Bali's most iconic attractions while observing the monkeys in their natural environment.

Continue your cultural exploration with a visit to Batuan Temple, one of Bali's oldest and most historically significant Hindu temples. Dating back over a thousand years, this beautifully preserved temple showcases traditional Balinese architecture, intricate stone carvings, sacred shrines, and peaceful courtyards. Learn about Bali's spiritual traditions while admiring the remarkable craftsmanship that has been preserved for generations.

Whether you're seeking an adrenaline-filled adventure, unforgettable wildlife encounters, or an authentic cultural experience, the ATV Quad Bike, Monkey Forest & Batuan Temple Tour offers the perfect blend of excitement and discovery — all in one incredible day.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous ATV riding experience?+

No. This tour is suitable for beginners and experienced riders alike. Professional instructors will provide safety instructions and basic riding guidance before the adventure begins.

Can two people ride one ATV?+

Yes. Tandem ATVs are available for guests who prefer to ride together. Single ATVs are also available upon request.

Is the ATV activity safe?+

Yes. Safety is the highest priority. Helmets, boots, professional guides, and a comprehensive safety briefing are provided before every ride.

Are there shower facilities after the ATV ride?+

Yes. Clean shower facilities, changing rooms, and lockers are available at the ATV base camp.

Can I feed the monkeys at Ubud Monkey Forest?+

No. Visitors are advised not to feed or provoke the monkeys. Please follow the park guidelines and keep valuables securely stored, as the monkeys are naturally curious.

Is Batuan Temple suitable for all visitors?+

Yes. Visitors of all ages are welcome. Please wear respectful clothing covering your shoulders and knees when entering the temple. Sarongs can be borrowed if needed.

Are all entrance fees included?+

Yes. ATV admission, Monkey Forest entrance, Batuan Temple entrance, and all attraction fees are included in the all-inclusive package.

Can I customize the itinerary?+

Yes. As this is a private tour, minor adjustments to the itinerary can often be arranged depending on your preferences and available time.
