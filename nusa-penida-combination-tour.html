Best of West & East Nusa Penida Tour

Experience the very best of Nusa Penida in one unforgettable day, where dramatic limestone cliffs, pristine white-sand beaches, and breathtaking ocean views come together to create the ultimate island adventure. The Best of West & East Nusa Penida Tour is perfect for travelers who want to explore the island's most iconic landmarks without choosing between the west and east.

Your journey begins with a scenic fast boat ride from Sanur Harbor to Nusa Penida. Upon arrival, your private driver will take you on an incredible island exploration, visiting four of Nusa Penida's most famous destinations.

Start your adventure at the legendary Kelingking Beach, home to the world-famous T-Rex-shaped cliff overlooking the turquoise waters of the Indian Ocean. From the spectacular viewpoint, you'll enjoy breathtaking panoramic views and one of the most photographed landscapes in Indonesia.

Continue to Broken Beach (Pasih Uug), a remarkable natural rock formation where a giant limestone arch allows the ocean to flow into a beautiful circular lagoon. Surrounded by dramatic coastal cliffs, this unique geological wonder offers stunning scenery and incredible photo opportunities.

After lunch, journey to the eastern side of the island to discover the spectacular Diamond Beach. Famous for its powdery white sand, crystal-clear turquoise waters, and towering limestone cliffs, Diamond Beach is one of Nusa Penida's most breathtaking coastal destinations. From the viewpoint, you'll be rewarded with unforgettable ocean vistas, while adventurous visitors can descend the staircase to experience the beach up close.

Complete your island adventure at the iconic Rumah Pohon (Tree House), perched high above the coastline overlooking the breathtaking Thousand Islands (Raja Lima). This famous cliffside viewpoint offers spectacular panoramic views of the sparkling ocean, dramatic limestone formations, and lush tropical scenery, making it one of the most sought-after photography spots on the island.

Whether you're a photography enthusiast, nature lover, couple, family, or first-time visitor to Bali, the Best of West & East Nusa Penida Tour offers the perfect combination of spectacular scenery, iconic landmarks, and unforgettable island experiences — all in one incredible day.

Good to Know

  • This tour includes round-trip fast boat transfers between Bali (Sanur Harbor) and Nusa Penida.
  • The itinerary covers both the west and east sides of Nusa Penida, making it a full-day island adventure.
  • Moderate walking is required at most attractions, with stairs and uneven paths at Kelingking Beach, Diamond Beach, and Rumah Pohon.
  • Descending to Diamond Beach is optional and recommended only for visitors with a good level of fitness due to the steep staircase.
  • Kelingking Beach is visited from the viewpoint only. Hiking down to the beach is not included in the standard itinerary.
  • Some roads in Nusa Penida are narrow, steep, and uneven, but all transportation is provided by an experienced local driver.
  • Boat departure times may vary depending on sea and weather conditions.
  • The order of destinations may change to avoid crowds or accommodate operational and weather conditions.
  • We recommend wearing comfortable footwear, light clothing, and bringing sunscreen to fully enjoy the tour.
  • Early departure is essential to maximize your time exploring the island's top attractions.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are the fast boat tickets included?+

Yes. The package includes round-trip fast boat tickets between Sanur Harbor and Nusa Penida.

Is lunch included?+

Yes. A delicious lunch at a local restaurant is included as part of the tour package.

Can I hike down to Kelingking Beach?+

The standard itinerary includes a visit to the Kelingking Beach viewpoint only. Hiking down to the beach is not included, as it requires additional time and a high level of physical fitness.

Can I go down to Diamond Beach?+

Yes. Descending to Diamond Beach is optional. Please note that the staircase is steep, and visitors should wear suitable footwear and exercise caution.

Is the entrance to Rumah Pohon (Tree House) included?+

Yes. The entrance fee is included. Please be aware that there may be a short queue if you wish to take photos on the Tree House platform.

Is this tour suitable for children?+

Yes. Families with children are welcome. However, children should always be supervised, particularly near cliff viewpoints, staircases, and uneven walking paths.

What should I wear?+

We recommend lightweight clothing, comfortable walking shoes or sandals, sunglasses, sunscreen, and a hat. Don't forget your camera or smartphone to capture the incredible scenery.

What happens if the weather is bad?+

Your safety is our highest priority. If sea or weather conditions affect fast boat operations or access to certain attractions, the itinerary may be adjusted or the tour rescheduled in accordance with local safety regulations.
