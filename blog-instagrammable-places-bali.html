Travel Tips

20 Most Instagrammable Places in Bali

MyBroBali Travel Guide · 8 min read

Bali is one of the world's most photogenic destinations, offering a perfect combination of tropical landscapes, ancient temples, dramatic cliffs, and unique cultural experiences. From breathtaking sunrise views to hidden waterfalls and iconic Balinese architecture, the island provides endless opportunities to capture unforgettable moments.

Whether you are a photography enthusiast, a content creator, or simply want to bring home beautiful memories, these are some of the most Instagrammable places in Bali that should be on your travel list.

1. Lempuyang Temple (Gate of Heaven)

One of Bali's most iconic photo spots, Lempuyang Temple is famous for its traditional split gate framing the majestic Mount Agung in the background. The peaceful temple atmosphere combined with the stunning mountain view creates one of Bali's most recognizable images.

Best time to visit: Early morning before 6:00 AM to enjoy clearer views and shorter waiting times.

2. Tegallalang Rice Terrace

Located in Ubud, Tegallalang Rice Terrace showcases Bali's traditional Subak irrigation system surrounded by beautiful green landscapes. The layered rice fields create a stunning natural backdrop perfect for photography.

3. Bali Jungle Swing

Experience the famous Bali Jungle Swing and capture the feeling of flying above lush tropical forests. With views of valleys, palm trees, and rice terraces, this attraction has become one of Bali's most popular photography experiences.

4. Kelingking Beach, Nusa Penida

Known for its iconic cliff formation resembling a dinosaur, Kelingking Beach offers one of the most breathtaking viewpoints in Bali. The combination of turquoise ocean, white sand, and dramatic cliffs creates a truly unforgettable scene.

5. Handara Gate

Surrounded by misty mountains and tropical greenery, Handara Gate is a classic Balinese photo location. The traditional architecture and peaceful highland atmosphere make it a favorite stop for visitors exploring North Bali.

6. Ulun Danu Beratan Temple

Located on Lake Beratan, Ulun Danu Beratan Temple appears to float on the water when conditions are perfect. The reflection of the temple, calm lake, and surrounding mountains create a magical atmosphere.

7. Broken Beach, Nusa Penida

This unique natural attraction features a giant circular cliff opening connected to the ocean. The dramatic rock formation and deep blue water make Broken Beach one of the most impressive coastal viewpoints in Bali.

8. Angel's Billabong, Nusa Penida

Famous for its natural infinity pool-like appearance, Angel's Billabong offers incredible ocean views surrounded by volcanic rock formations. It is a beautiful photography spot when visited under safe conditions.

9. Tirta Gangga Water Palace

This former royal water palace in East Bali features elegant fountains, koi ponds, stone pathways, and beautiful gardens. The combination of traditional Balinese design and peaceful surroundings creates endless photo opportunities.

10. Taman Ujung Water Palace

With its grand architecture, large pools, and ocean views, Taman Ujung is one of East Bali's hidden gems. The unique blend of Balinese and European design makes it a favorite location for photographers.

11. Mount Batur Sunrise Viewpoint

Watching the sunrise from Mount Batur is one of Bali's most unforgettable experiences. The golden morning light over mountains, volcanoes, and Lake Batur creates a spectacular natural scene.

12. Uluwatu Temple

Perched on a dramatic cliff above the Indian Ocean, Uluwatu Temple offers incredible sunset views. The combination of ancient temple architecture, ocean scenery, and the famous Kecak Fire Dance creates a magical atmosphere.

13. Melasti Beach

Located beneath impressive limestone cliffs, Melasti Beach features crystal-clear water, white sand, and beautiful coastal views. It is one of South Bali's best locations for beach photography.

14. Sekumpul Waterfall

Often considered one of Bali's most beautiful waterfalls, Sekumpul Waterfall is surrounded by lush jungle and towering cliffs. The journey to reach it adds to the adventure and creates amazing nature photography opportunities.

15. Banyumala Twin Waterfalls

Hidden in North Bali's highlands, Banyumala Twin Waterfalls offers a peaceful tropical setting with a clear natural pool surrounded by greenery. It is a perfect spot for nature lovers and photographers.

16. Campuhan Ridge Walk

Located in Ubud, Campuhan Ridge Walk offers a peaceful walking trail surrounded by green hills and beautiful countryside views. Early morning and sunset are the best times for photography.

17. Bali Swing at Aloha / Tegalalang Area

The famous swing attractions around Ubud provide panoramic views of jungle valleys, rice terraces, and tropical landscapes, making them popular among travelers looking for unique Bali photos.

18. GWK Cultural Park

Home to the massive Garuda Wisnu Kencana Statue, GWK Cultural Park combines impressive architecture, cultural performances, and panoramic views of southern Bali.

19. Tanah Lot Temple

Built on a rock formation surrounded by the sea, Tanah Lot Temple is one of Bali's most famous landmarks. The sunset views create a dramatic and unforgettable photo opportunity.

20. Bali Traditional Villages & Local Experiences

Beyond famous attractions, Bali's traditional villages offer authentic photography experiences through local culture, ceremonies, temples, traditional markets, and everyday Balinese life.

Best Time for Photography in Bali

For the best photos, consider visiting attractions during:

Early Morning

  • Fewer crowds
  • Softer lighting
  • Cooler temperatures

Golden Hour (Sunrise & Sunset)

  • Warm natural colors
  • Beautiful shadows
  • Perfect atmosphere for landscape photography

Photography Tips for Bali

  • Visit popular attractions early to avoid crowds.
  • Wear comfortable clothing suitable for walking.
  • Bring sunscreen and water, especially for outdoor locations.
  • Use a waterproof phone case for beach and waterfall areas.
  • Respect local customs when photographing temples and ceremonies.
  • Always follow safety instructions, especially near cliffs and ocean areas.

Final Thoughts

Bali is a destination where every corner offers a beautiful story to capture. From ancient temples and dramatic cliffs to tropical forests and hidden waterfalls, the island provides countless opportunities for unforgettable photographs.

Whether you are creating travel memories, capturing content for social media, or simply enjoying Bali's natural beauty, exploring these 20 Most Instagrammable Places in Bali will make your journey even more special.
