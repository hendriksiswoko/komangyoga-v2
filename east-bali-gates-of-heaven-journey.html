About this tour

Embark on a captivating journey through the cultural and historical treasures of East Bali with our East Bali – Gates of Heaven Journey. This full-day private tour showcases some of Bali's most iconic landmarks, where ancient temples, royal water palaces, and breathtaking mountain scenery come together to create an unforgettable experience.

Your adventure begins with a visit to the legendary Lempuyang Temple, home to the world-famous Gate of Heaven. Perched high on the slopes of Mount Lempuyang, this sacred temple offers spectacular panoramic views of Mount Agung, Bali's highest volcano. Capture the iconic reflection photo at the Gate of Heaven while experiencing the peaceful spiritual atmosphere of one of Bali's oldest and most revered temples.

Continue your journey to the beautiful Tirta Gangga Water Palace, a former royal palace renowned for its elegant fountains, crystal-clear ponds, stepping stones, and lush tropical gardens. Stroll through this tranquil oasis while admiring traditional Balinese architecture and the palace's beautifully landscaped grounds.

Your final destination is the magnificent Taman Ujung Water Palace, once the royal retreat of the Karangasem Kingdom. Surrounded by expansive gardens, scenic bridges, and picturesque pools, this historic palace combines Balinese, European, and Chinese architectural influences, making it one of the most photogenic locations in East Bali.

Whether you're passionate about photography, fascinated by Balinese culture, or simply looking to explore Bali's most iconic landmarks, the East Bali – Gates of Heaven Journey offers the perfect combination of history, spirituality, and natural beauty.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the Gate of Heaven photo included?+

Yes. Visitors can take the famous Gate of Heaven photo with the assistance of the local temple photographers. Please note that waiting times may vary during busy periods.

Why should we leave early?+

Lempuyang Temple is one of Bali's most popular attractions. Early departure helps reduce waiting times and provides better lighting for photography.

Are temple entrance fees included?+

Yes. All entrance fees are included in the all-inclusive package.

Is this tour suitable for children?+

Yes. The tour is suitable for families and travelers of all ages, although moderate walking and climbing stairs are required.

Can I wear shorts when visiting the temple?+

Visitors should wear respectful clothing that covers the shoulders and knees. If needed, a sarong is typically provided at the temple entrance.
