Ubud

Ultimate Ubud Travel Guide (2026 Edition)

MyBroBali Travel Guide · 6 min read

Nestled in the heart of Bali, Ubud is a destination that perfectly blends culture, nature, wellness, and adventure. Known as Bali's cultural capital, Ubud attracts millions of visitors each year with its breathtaking rice terraces, sacred temples, traditional arts, and vibrant local markets. Whether you're visiting Bali for the first time or returning to explore more, Ubud offers an unforgettable experience for every traveler.

Best Things to Do in Ubud

Ubud is home to some of Bali's most iconic attractions. Wander through the lush Tegalalang Rice Terrace, experience a traditional purification ritual at Tirta Empul Holy Spring Temple, explore the historic Ubud Royal Palace, and shop for handcrafted souvenirs at the Ubud Art Market. For nature lovers, the famous Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary and the scenic Campuhan Ridge Walk are must-visit destinations.

If you're looking for adventure, Ubud also offers exciting activities such as ATV Quad Bike rides, Ayung River White Water Rafting, and the popular Bali Jungle Swing, all surrounded by stunning tropical landscapes.

Best Time to Visit Ubud

Ubud can be visited year-round, but the dry season (April to October) is generally the best time to explore its outdoor attractions. During these months, you'll enjoy sunny weather, comfortable temperatures, and ideal conditions for sightseeing, trekking, and adventure activities.

The rainy season (November to March) transforms Ubud into an even greener paradise, offering fewer crowds and beautiful scenery, making it an excellent choice for travellers seeking a more peaceful experience.

Local Food and Coffee

No visit to Ubud is complete without experiencing its local cuisine. From authentic Balinese dishes such as Babi Guling, Nasi Campur, and Sate Lilit to healthy cafés and international restaurants, Ubud offers something for every taste.

Many visitors also enjoy stopping at a traditional Bali Coffee Plantation, where they can learn about local coffee production and sample a variety of Balinese coffees and herbal teas.

Travel Tips

To make the most of your visit:

  • Start your day early to avoid crowds at popular attractions.
  • Wear comfortable clothing and walking shoes.
  • Carry cash for entrance fees and local markets.
  • Dress respectfully when visiting temples.
  • Stay hydrated, especially during outdoor activities.

Why Visit Ubud?

Ubud offers much more than beautiful scenery. It is a destination where visitors can experience Bali's rich culture, breathtaking nature, exciting adventures, and warm hospitality—all in one place. Whether you're exploring ancient temples, enjoying thrilling outdoor activities, or simply relaxing in the peaceful countryside, Ubud promises memories that will last a lifetime.
