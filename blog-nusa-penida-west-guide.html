Nusa Penida

Ultimate Nusa Penida West Tour Guide

MyBroBali Travel Guide · 5 min read

Just a short boat ride from mainland Bali, Nusa Penida is one of Indonesia's most spectacular island destinations. Famous for its dramatic limestone cliffs, crystal-clear waters, and breathtaking coastal scenery, the island has become a must-visit destination for travelers seeking unforgettable natural beauty.

The West Nusa Penida Tour is the most popular way to explore the island, taking you to its most iconic landmarks in a single day.

Discover Nusa Penida's Most Famous Attractions

Your journey begins at the world-renowned Kelingking Beach, often recognized as one of the most photographed locations in Bali. Its dramatic cliff, shaped like a dinosaur, overlooks turquoise waters and a pristine white-sand beach far below, offering one of the island's most breathtaking viewpoints.

Continue to Broken Beach (Pasih Uug), a unique natural rock formation where a circular cliff surrounds the ocean, creating a stunning natural arch. Just a few minutes away is Angel's Billabong, a beautiful natural infinity pool formed by volcanic rock. During low tide, visitors can admire its crystal-clear water and incredible coastal scenery.

The final stop is Crystal Bay, a peaceful beach known for its calm waters, soft white sand, and spectacular ocean views. It's the perfect place to relax, enjoy the tropical atmosphere, or simply unwind before returning to Bali.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to explore West Nusa Penida is during the dry season (April to October), when the weather is sunny and the ocean conditions are generally calmer. Early departures from Bali are highly recommended, allowing you to maximize your time on the island and avoid the busiest crowds at popular viewpoints.

Travel Tips

Wear lightweight clothing, comfortable walking shoes, sunscreen, sunglasses, and a hat, as most attractions have limited shade. Bring a reusable water bottle, a camera, and cash for entrance fees or personal expenses. Some viewpoints feature uneven paths and steep cliffs, so always stay within designated safety areas and follow local guidelines.

Why Visit West Nusa Penida?

West Nusa Penida offers some of Bali's most iconic landscapes, making it the perfect destination for nature lovers, photographers, couples, and first-time visitors. From towering cliffs and unique rock formations to beautiful beaches and crystal-clear waters, every stop reveals a different side of this extraordinary island.
