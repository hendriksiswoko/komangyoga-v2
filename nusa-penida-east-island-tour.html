About this tour

Explore the untouched beauty of Nusa Penida's eastern coastline, where dramatic cliffs, pristine white-sand beaches, and breathtaking ocean views create one of Bali's most unforgettable island experiences. The Nusa Penida East Island Tour is perfect for travelers seeking spectacular scenery, hidden gems, and iconic photography spots.

Your adventure begins with a scenic fast boat ride from Sanur Harbor to Nusa Penida. Upon arrival, your private driver will take you on an unforgettable journey through the island's most picturesque eastern attractions.

Start your exploration at the world-famous Diamond Beach, renowned for its dazzling white sand, crystal-clear turquoise waters, and towering limestone cliffs. From the viewpoint, you'll enjoy one of the most iconic panoramas in Indonesia, while adventurous visitors can descend the staircase to experience the beach up close.

Next, visit the tranquil Atuh Beach, a secluded paradise surrounded by dramatic rock formations and gentle waves. This hidden beach offers a peaceful atmosphere, making it the perfect place to relax, admire the scenery, and capture stunning photographs.

Complete your journey at the famous Rumah Pohon (Tree House), one of Nusa Penida's most iconic landmarks. Perched high on the cliffside overlooking the ocean, the Tree House offers breathtaking panoramic views of the coastline and the nearby Thousand Islands. It is one of the island's most sought-after photo locations and an unforgettable highlight of your visit.

Whether you're a photography enthusiast, nature lover, or simply looking to experience the untouched beauty of Nusa Penida, the Nusa Penida East Island Tour promises an extraordinary adventure filled with spectacular landscapes and unforgettable memories.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are the fast boat tickets included?+

Yes. Return fast boat tickets between Sanur and Nusa Penida are included in the all-inclusive package.

Can I go down to Diamond Beach?+

Yes. Descending to Diamond Beach is optional. Please note that the staircase is steep and requires a moderate level of fitness.

Is the Tree House entrance included?+

Yes. The entrance fee is included in the all-inclusive package. Please note that there may be a queue if you wish to take photos on the Tree House platform.

Is this tour suitable for children?+

Yes. However, children should always be supervised by an adult, especially near cliff edges and stairways.

What happens if the weather is bad?+

The tour operates in most weather conditions. If sea conditions affect the fast boat schedule, departure times may be adjusted or the tour may be rescheduled for safety reasons.
