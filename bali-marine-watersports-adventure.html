About this tour

Get ready for an unforgettable day of excitement with our Bali Marine Watersports Adventure. Set against the crystal-clear waters of Bali's southern coastline, this thrilling experience offers the perfect combination of adrenaline, fun, and tropical scenery. Whether you're soaring high above the ocean, speeding across the waves, or exploring the underwater world, this package is designed to create memories that will last a lifetime.

Choose from three exciting watersport combinations, each carefully selected to offer a unique mix of adventure and excitement.

Feel the thrill of Parasailing Adventure, where you'll glide high above the sparkling ocean while enjoying breathtaking panoramic views of Bali's coastline. It's an unforgettable flying experience suitable for beginners and adventure lovers alike.

Experience the excitement of Jet Ski, where you'll race across the waves under the supervision of professional instructors, or enjoy the laughter-filled fun of the Banana Boat, one of Bali's most popular group activities.

Looking for something even more unique? Walk along the ocean floor with the incredible Sea Walker experience, allowing you to discover colorful tropical fish and marine life without needing scuba diving certification. Or challenge yourself with the exhilarating Fly Board, where powerful water jets lift you high above the sea for an unforgettable adrenaline rush.

Whether you're traveling with friends, your partner, or family, the Bali Marine Watersports Adventure offers the perfect combination of fun, excitement, and unforgettable ocean experiences.

What's included & excluded

  • Safety briefing before every activity
  • Complete safety equipment
  • Life jacket for all participants
  • Locker facilities
  • Shower facilities
  • Lunch
  • Bottled mineral water
  • Insurance coverage during watersport activities

What's Excluded

  • Personal expenses
  • Additional watersport activities
  • Professional photo or video services
  • Towels
  • Tips and gratuities (optional)
  • Transportation outside the included pickup areas
  • Optional activities not included in the selected package

What to Bring

  • Swimwear
  • Change of clothes
  • Towel
  • Sunscreen
  • Sunglasses
  • Waterproof phone case
  • Waterproof action camera (optional)
  • Flip-flops or sandals

Good to Know

  • No previous watersport experience is required.
  • Professional instructors will provide a full safety briefing before each activity.
  • All safety equipment, including life jackets, is provided and must be worn during applicable activities.
  • Minimum age and health restrictions may apply for certain activities such as Fly Board and Jet Ski.
  • Activities are subject to sea, weather, and tide conditions for participant safety.
  • The order of activities may vary depending on operational conditions.
  • Locker and shower facilities are available for your convenience.
  • Hotel transfers are available from Nusa Dua, Kuta, Legian, Seminyak, and Ubud.
  • Participants should be in good physical condition and inform staff of any medical conditions before participating.

Frequently asked questions

No. All activities are suitable for beginners, and professional instructors will guide you throughout the experience.

Is lunch included?+

Yes. Lunch is included in all watersport packages.

Are lockers and showers available?+

Yes. Clean locker and shower facilities are available for all participants.

Can children participate?+

Some activities are suitable for children, while others have minimum age or height requirements. Please contact us for specific recommendations based on your group.

Absolutely. All activities are supervised by trained professionals, and certified safety equipment is provided.

What happens if the weather is bad?+

For your safety, activities may be delayed, modified, or rescheduled if sea or weather conditions are not suitable.

Can I book more than one package?+

Yes. You may combine packages or add additional activities depending on availability. Please contact us for a customized itinerary.
