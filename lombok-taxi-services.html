Lombok Taxi Services

A seamless, comfortable journey to every corner of Lombok.

Exploring the beautiful island of Lombok has never been easier with our Taxi Service, designed to provide you with a seamless and comfortable journey from the moment you arrive. Whether you're flying into Lombok International Airport or already on the island, our professional and friendly drivers are ready to transport you to your destination safely and on time.

Airport Pick-Up Service

Our Airport Pick-Up service is tailored to ensure your arrival in Lombok is stress-free. Upon landing, you will be greeted by our driver with a personalized name sign, so you won't have to worry about navigating the busy airport. We provide full assistance with your luggage, allowing you to relax after your flight while we take care of everything.

Whether you're heading to the popular beaches of Kuta Lombok, the stunning resorts in Senggigi, or planning a ferry transfer to the Gili Islands, we ensure a smooth transition from the airport to your accommodation.

Flexible Transport Across Lombok

In addition to airport transfers, we offer flexible taxi services to all parts of Lombok. From vibrant markets to secluded beaches, and from cultural landmarks to thrilling adventure spots, we can take you wherever you wish to explore. Our drivers are knowledgeable about the island and can suggest the best routes or offer insights into hidden gems that you may want to visit.

Some of our popular destinations include:

  • Kuta Lombok — a favorite among surfers, known for its pristine beaches and stunning views.
  • Senggigi — famous for its scenic coastline and relaxing atmosphere.
  • Bangsal Harbor — the gateway to the Gili Islands, where we provide easy transfer services for your island-hopping adventures.
  • Tetebatu Village — a hidden gem where you can experience local culture and breathtaking landscapes.

We are also happy to offer customized day tours for those who want to explore Lombok at their own pace. Whether it's a trip to the lush waterfalls of Benang Kelambu, a visit to the traditional Sasak villages, or an adventure up Mount Rinjani, we can arrange it all.
