About this tour

Experience the cultural heart of Bali on this unforgettable full-day journey through Ubud, the island's artistic and spiritual centre. This carefully curated tour combines breathtaking natural landscapes, sacred temples, royal heritage, and authentic local experiences, making it the perfect introduction to Bali's unique culture.

Begin your day with a visit to a Bali Coffee Plantation, where you'll discover the traditional process of coffee making and enjoy complimentary tastings of locally produced coffee and herbal teas. Continue to the world-famous Tegalalang Rice Terrace, renowned for its stunning emerald-green rice paddies and the traditional Balinese Subak irrigation system, recognized as a UNESCO Cultural Landscape.

Next, visit the sacred Tirta Empul Holy Spring Temple, one of Bali's most important Hindu temples, where locals and visitors gather to participate in traditional purification rituals using the natural holy spring water. Continue your cultural exploration at the historic Ubud Royal Palace, a beautiful example of traditional Balinese architecture and the former residence of Ubud's royal family.

Complete your journey at the vibrant Ubud Art Market, where you'll find a wide variety of handmade souvenirs, local artwork, textiles, wood carvings, and unique Balinese handicrafts — perfect for bringing home a piece of Bali's rich artistic heritage.

Whether you're visiting Bali for the first time or returning to explore its cultural treasures, the Best of Ubud Tour offers an authentic and memorable experience that showcases the very best of Ubud.
