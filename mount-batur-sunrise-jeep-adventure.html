About this tour

Witness one of Bali's most spectacular sunrises without the need for hiking. The Mount Batur Sunrise Jeep Adventure offers an exciting off-road journey across the volcanic landscape of Mount Batur, making it the perfect choice for travellers seeking breathtaking scenery with maximum comfort.

Your adventure begins in the early morning as you travel by 4WD Jeep to one of Mount Batur's best sunrise viewpoints. As the sun rises above the surrounding mountains, enjoy a delicious breakfast while taking in panoramic views of the active volcano, Lake Batur, and the peaceful morning mist.

Continue your adventure across the dramatic Black Lava fields, created by Mount Batur's historic volcanic eruptions. The unique volcanic terrain provides incredible opportunities for sightseeing and photography while experiencing the raw beauty of Bali's volcanic landscape.

After your jeep adventure, relax and rejuvenate at the Batur Natural Hot Spring, where you can soak in naturally heated mineral-rich pools overlooking the stunning Lake Batur. Complete your journey with a visit to a traditional Bali Coffee Plantation, where you'll discover the art of Balinese coffee production and enjoy complimentary tastings of locally grown coffee and herbal teas.

Whether you're traveling as a couple, with family, or in a group, this tour combines adventure, relaxation, and unforgettable scenery, making it one of Bali's most popular experiences.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to hike Mount Batur?+

No. This tour is designed for guests who want to experience the sunrise comfortably by 4WD Jeep without hiking.

Is breakfast included?+

Yes. A light breakfast is served while you enjoy the sunrise at Mount Batur.

Can children join this tour?+

Yes. The tour is suitable for children and families, provided children are accompanied by an adult.

Should I bring swimwear?+

Yes. We recommend bringing swimwear, a towel, and a change of clothes if you plan to enjoy the Batur Natural Hot Spring.

What if the weather is cloudy?+

The tour operates in most weather conditions. While sunrise visibility cannot be guaranteed, the volcanic landscape and overall experience remain enjoyable throughout the year.
