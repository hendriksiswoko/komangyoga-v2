About this tour

Experience the perfect blend of adventure and Bali's natural beauty with our Ubud Adventure Package. Designed for thrill-seekers and nature lovers alike, this full-day experience takes you through Ubud's lush tropical landscapes with exciting outdoor activities and breathtaking scenery.

Begin your adventure with an exhilarating ATV Quad Bike ride, navigating through jungle trails, rice fields, muddy tracks, and traditional villages. Continue the excitement with an unforgettable Ayung River Rafting experience, where you'll paddle through Bali's longest river surrounded by stunning cliffs, waterfalls, and lush rainforest.

After the adrenaline-filled activities, unwind with a visit to a Bali Coffee Plantation, where you'll learn about traditional coffee-making processes and enjoy a selection of locally produced coffee and herbal teas. Complete your day with a memorable ride on the Bali Jungle Swing, soaring high above the tropical forest while taking in spectacular panoramic views — an ideal spot for capturing unforgettable photos.

Whether you're traveling with friends, family, or your partner, this package offers the perfect combination of adventure, nature, and authentic Balinese experiences in one unforgettable journey.
