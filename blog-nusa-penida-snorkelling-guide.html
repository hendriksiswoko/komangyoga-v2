Nusa Penida

Ultimate Nusa Penida Snorkelling Guide

MyBroBali Travel Guide · 5 min read

Nusa Penida is widely recognized as one of the best snorkelling destinations in Bali, offering crystal-clear waters, vibrant coral reefs, and an incredible variety of marine life. Whether you're a first-time snorkeler or an experienced ocean enthusiast, the island provides an unforgettable underwater adventure.

A typical West Nusa Penida Snorkelling Tour combines scenic island sightseeing with visits to some of the region's most famous snorkelling spots, including Manta Point, Crystal Bay, Gamat Bay, and Wall Point.

Discover Nusa Penida's Best Snorkelling Spots

Your adventure begins with an exciting boat trip to some of the island's most spectacular marine locations.

Manta Point is the highlight of the tour, where visitors have the opportunity to snorkel alongside the majestic reef manta rays in their natural habitat. While sightings cannot be guaranteed, this location offers one of the best chances in Bali to encounter these gentle giants.

Next, explore Crystal Bay, known for its calm, clear waters and colorful coral reefs. The excellent visibility makes it an ideal spot for beginners and underwater photography.

Continue to Gamat Bay, a hidden gem featuring healthy coral gardens and an abundance of tropical fish. Its calm conditions and rich marine biodiversity make it one of the island's favorite snorkelling locations.

Finally, visit Wall Point, where vibrant coral formations cling to dramatic underwater walls, creating a beautiful environment for observing marine life in deeper waters.

After your snorkelling adventure, the tour continues to the iconic Kelingking Beach, where you'll enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of Nusa Penida's most famous cliffside landmark.

Best Time to Visit

The best time for snorkelling in Nusa Penida is during the dry season (April to October), when the sea is generally calmer and underwater visibility is at its best. Morning departures are recommended, as ocean conditions are usually more favourable earlier in the day.

Although manta rays can be seen throughout the year, wildlife sightings depend on natural conditions and can never be guaranteed.

Travel Tips

For the best snorkelling experience, bring:

  • Swimwear
  • Towel
  • Reef-safe sunscreen
  • Sunglasses and a hat
  • Waterproof phone case or action camera
  • Change of clothes
  • Motion sickness medication (if you're sensitive to boat travel)

Always follow your guide's instructions, wear the provided life jacket when required, and avoid touching coral reefs or marine animals to help protect the underwater ecosystem.

Why Visit Nusa Penida for Snorkelling?

Nusa Penida offers some of the richest marine biodiversity in Bali, making it a dream destination for ocean lovers. From swimming in crystal-clear waters and exploring vibrant coral reefs to the unforgettable chance of encountering manta rays, every snorkelling stop offers a unique glimpse into Bali's incredible underwater world.
