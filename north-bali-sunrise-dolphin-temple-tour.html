About this tour

Experience one of Bali's most unforgettable mornings with our North Bali Sunrise Dolphin & Temple Tour. This full-day adventure combines an incredible dolphin-watching experience at sunrise with breathtaking mountain scenery and one of Bali's most iconic lakeside temples.

Your journey begins before dawn with an early hotel pick-up as you travel to the peaceful coastal village of Lovina, famous for its calm waters and resident pods of wild dolphins.

Board a traditional Balinese outrigger boat and cruise across the tranquil sea as the first rays of sunlight illuminate the horizon. Watch in awe as playful dolphins swim and leap through the sparkling waters in their natural habitat, creating an unforgettable sunrise experience. The calm morning atmosphere and stunning ocean views make this one of Bali's most magical wildlife encounters.

After the dolphin tour, continue your journey into Bali's cool mountain highlands to visit Wanagiri Hidden Hill, one of the island's most scenic viewpoints. Overlooking the beautiful twin lakes of Lake Buyan and Lake Tamblingan, Wanagiri is famous for its iconic photo spots, including giant bird nests, swings, and unique wooden platforms that provide spectacular panoramic views of the surrounding rainforest and lakes.

Complete your adventure with a visit to the magnificent Ulun Danu Beratan Temple, one of Bali's most photographed and sacred landmarks. Gracefully floating on the shores of Lake Beratan, this beautiful 17th-century Hindu temple is dedicated to Dewi Danu, the goddess of lakes and water. Surrounded by misty mountains and peaceful gardens, the temple offers a truly serene atmosphere and a wonderful insight into Bali's rich cultural and spiritual heritage.

Whether you're hoping to witness wild dolphins at sunrise, capture breathtaking mountain scenery, or explore one of Bali's most iconic temples, the North Bali Sunrise Dolphin & Temple Tour offers the perfect combination of wildlife, nature, and culture in one unforgettable journey.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What time does the tour start?+

Hotel pick-up usually begins between 2:30 AM and 3:00 AM, depending on your accommodation location, to ensure you arrive in Lovina before sunrise.

Are dolphin sightings guaranteed?+

No. Dolphins are wild animals, so sightings cannot be guaranteed. However, Lovina is one of the best places in Bali to observe dolphins, and sightings are very frequent.

Is the traditional boat ride included?+

Yes. The package includes a traditional Balinese outrigger boat and all necessary safety equipment.

Are entrance fees included?+

Yes. All entrance tickets to Wanagiri Hidden Hill and Ulun Danu Beratan Temple are included in the tour price.

Is this tour suitable for children?+

Yes. This tour is suitable for guests of all ages. Children should always be accompanied by an adult during the boat ride and while visiting attractions.

What should I wear?+

We recommend comfortable clothing, a light jacket for the cool morning weather, comfortable walking shoes, and sun protection for later in the day.

What happens if the weather is bad?+

For your safety, the boat trip or itinerary may be delayed, modified, or rescheduled if sea or weather conditions are unsuitable.

Can I customize the itinerary?+

Yes. As this is a private tour, minor itinerary adjustments may be possible depending on your preferences and time availability.
