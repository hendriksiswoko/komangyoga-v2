About this tour

Capture the beauty of Bali at its most iconic and photogenic destinations with our Bali Instagram Tour. Perfect for photography enthusiasts, couples, honeymooners, and social media lovers, this full-day private tour takes you to some of the island's most breathtaking landscapes, cultural landmarks, and hidden natural wonders.

Your adventure begins with a visit to the world-famous Lempuyang Temple, home to the iconic Gate of Heaven. Set against the majestic backdrop of Mount Agung, this sacred temple offers one of Bali's most recognizable photo opportunities while providing a glimpse into the island's rich spiritual heritage.

Continue to the elegant Tirta Gangga Water Palace, a former royal palace featuring beautiful fountains, koi ponds, stepping stones, and lush tropical gardens. This peaceful retreat offers countless picture-perfect corners surrounded by traditional Balinese architecture.

Next, journey to the magical Tukad Cepung Waterfall, one of Bali's most unique hidden waterfalls. Sunlight streaming through the cave-like cliffs creates a breathtaking natural spotlight, making this location one of the island's most spectacular photography spots.

Your tour continues to the famous Tegalalang Rice Terrace, where endless layers of vibrant green rice paddies showcase Bali's traditional Subak irrigation system. Enjoy panoramic views, explore scenic walking paths, and immerse yourself in the tranquil beauty of Bali's countryside.

Complete your experience with an optional Bali Swing, where you can soar high above the lush jungle and rice terraces while capturing unforgettable photos with breathtaking panoramic views.

Whether you're creating unforgettable travel memories or searching for Bali's most Instagram-worthy locations, the Bali Instagram Tour offers the perfect combination of culture, nature, and stunning photography opportunities.

Good to Know

  • Early hotel pick-up is recommended to minimize waiting time at Lempuyang Temple.
  • Waiting times for the famous Gate of Heaven photo may vary during peak seasons.
  • The reflection effect in the Gate of Heaven photos is created by local photographers using a mirror.
  • Moderate walking is required, including stairs at Tukad Cepung Waterfall and uneven paths at Tegalalang Rice Terrace.
  • Please dress respectfully when visiting temples. Sarongs are available if required.
  • The itinerary may be adjusted depending on weather and traffic conditions.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Bali Swing included in the package?+

The Bali Swing is available as an optional add-on and can be included upon request.

Why do we start early?+

Lempuyang Temple is one of Bali's busiest attractions. An early departure helps reduce waiting time and provides the best lighting for photography.

Are all entrance fees included?+

Yes. All attraction entrance fees are included in the all-inclusive package. The Bali Swing is optional and charged separately.

Is this tour suitable for children?+

Yes. This tour is suitable for guests of all ages, although some attractions involve moderate walking and stairs.

Can I customize the itinerary?+

Yes. As this is a private tour, minor adjustments to the itinerary can be arranged based on your preferences and time availability.
