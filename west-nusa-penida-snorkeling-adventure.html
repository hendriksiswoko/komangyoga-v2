About this tour

Experience the best of Nusa Penida above and below the water with our West Nusa Penida + Snorkeling Adventure. This exciting full-day tour combines world-class snorkeling with one of Bali's most iconic island destinations.

Your adventure begins with an early morning hotel pickup before heading to Sanur Harbor, where you'll board a fast boat bound for the beautiful island of Nusa Penida.

Upon arrival, you'll head straight to the harbor for an unforgettable snorkeling adventure through the crystal-clear waters surrounding the island.

Your first stop is Manta Point, one of Bali's most famous snorkeling sites and the best place to encounter the gentle giant manta rays in their natural habitat. While sightings are common, they can never be guaranteed because these magnificent creatures are completely wild.

Continue to Gamat Bay, a stunning snorkeling location known for its colorful coral gardens, crystal-clear visibility, and countless tropical fish.

Next, explore Wall Point, where dramatic underwater reef walls create one of the richest marine ecosystems around Nusa Penida. Keep an eye out for schools of tropical fish, vibrant corals, sea turtles, and other fascinating marine life.

Your final snorkeling destination is the beautiful Crystal Bay, famous for its calm waters and thriving coral reefs. Relax, swim, and enjoy the incredible underwater scenery before returning to shore.

After snorkeling, enjoy a delicious lunch at a local restaurant before continuing your island exploration.

The afternoon is dedicated to the legendary Kelingking Beach, home to the iconic T-Rex-shaped cliff that has become one of Indonesia's most photographed landmarks. From the viewpoint, you'll enjoy spectacular panoramic views of towering limestone cliffs, turquoise waters, and the endless Indian Ocean — perfect for unforgettable holiday photos.

After an amazing day exploring one of Bali's most beautiful islands, you'll return to the harbor for your fast boat journey back to Sanur, where your driver will be waiting to transfer you comfortably back to your hotel.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I swim with manta rays?+

Yes. If manta rays are present at Manta Point, you'll have the opportunity to snorkel alongside them. However, sightings cannot be guaranteed as they are wild animals.

Do I need snorkelling experience?+

No. This tour is suitable for beginners. Professional snorkelling guides and life jackets are provided to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience.

Are snorkelling equipment and fast boat tickets included?+

Yes. The package includes complete snorkelling equipment, round-trip fast boat tickets, and all necessary transfers.

Will we hike down to Kelingking Beach?+

No. The tour includes a visit to the Kelingking Beach viewpoint, allowing you to enjoy the spectacular scenery and take photos without the strenuous hike to the beach.

What happens if sea conditions are unsafe?+

Your safety is our top priority. If sea conditions are not suitable for snorkeling, the snorkeling locations or itinerary may be adjusted, or the tour may be rescheduled based on the operator's safety guidelines.
