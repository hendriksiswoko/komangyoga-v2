Ultimate Ubud Adventure Guide: ATV, Rafting & Jungle Swing

MyBroBali Travel Guide · 5 min read

Ubud is more than just Bali's cultural heart—it's also the island's top destination for outdoor adventure. Surrounded by lush jungles, scenic rice terraces, winding rivers, and traditional villages, Ubud offers the perfect combination of excitement and natural beauty.

If you're looking to experience the best of Bali in a single day, combining an ATV Quad Bike Adventure, Ayung River White Water Rafting, and the iconic Bali Jungle Swing is one of the most popular choices among international travellers.

ATV Quad Bike Adventure

Start your day with an exciting ATV ride through Ubud's diverse landscapes. Navigate muddy tracks, jungle trails, rice fields, rivers, and hidden tunnels while enjoying Bali's beautiful countryside. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced rider, professional guides and safety equipment ensure a fun and safe experience for everyone.

Ayung River White Water Rafting

Continue your adventure on the famous Ayung River, Bali's longest and most scenic river. Paddle through gentle rapids surrounded by tropical rainforest, waterfalls, and impressive stone carvings along the river cliffs. Suitable for beginners and families, rafting on the Ayung River offers the perfect balance of adventure and breathtaking scenery.

Bali Jungle Swing

Complete your journey with a ride on the famous Bali Jungle Swing. Swing high above the lush jungle while taking in spectacular panoramic views of Ubud's tropical landscape. It's one of Bali's most iconic photo spots and a must-visit destination for anyone looking to capture unforgettable memories.

Why Choose This Adventure?

Combining these three activities allows you to experience the very best of Ubud in one unforgettable day. From thrilling off-road adventures and scenic river rafting to breathtaking jungle views, this tour offers the perfect mix of adrenaline, nature, and relaxation.

Whether you're traveling with friends, family, or your partner, the Ultimate Ubud Adventure is an experience that showcases the adventurous side of Bali while creating memories that will last a lifetime.
