Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose any destinations in Bali?+

Yes. You are free to choose your preferred attractions, and we'll help create the most efficient route for your day.

How many places can I visit in one day?+

We recommend 4–6 destinations for a full-day tour, depending on travel distances and the time you wish to spend at each location.

Can I combine sightseeing and activities?+

Absolutely. You can mix cultural attractions, waterfalls, beaches, adventure activities, shopping, dining, and more into a single itinerary.

Are entrance tickets included?+

By default, entrance tickets are not included. However, we also offer all-inclusive custom tour packages upon request.

Can you recommend an itinerary if I'm not sure where to go?+

Yes! Our local experts are happy to suggest the best itinerary based on your interests, whether you're looking for culture, nature, adventure, romance, family-friendly activities, or hidden gems.
