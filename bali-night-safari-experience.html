About this tour

Step into the wild after sunset with our Bali Night Safari Experience, an unforgettable evening adventure that brings you closer than ever to some of the world's most magnificent wildlife. Unlike a traditional zoo visit, this exclusive night safari allows you to witness animals in their natural nocturnal behavior while enjoying thrilling encounters, live entertainment, and a delicious BBQ dinner.

Your adventure begins with a comfortable hotel pick-up before arriving at Bali Safari Park, where you'll be welcomed with a refreshing welcome drink. As the sun sets, prepare yourself for a truly unique wildlife experience unlike any other in Bali.

Begin your evening with a Walking Safari, where you'll stroll through specially designed pathways to observe fascinating wildlife and learn about conservation efforts while enjoying the peaceful nighttime atmosphere of the park.

The highlight of your visit is the incredible Night Safari Journey aboard a specially designed caged tram. As you travel through the safari, you'll come face-to-face with a variety of free-roaming animals including zebras, elephants, deer, and giraffes. One of the most thrilling moments is witnessing majestic lions and powerful tigers roaming freely just outside your tram, creating an unforgettable close-range wildlife encounter.

During the safari journey, you'll also have the opportunity to hand-feed selected herbivorous animals under the supervision of experienced park rangers, making this a memorable experience for both adults and children.

After your safari adventure, enjoy a delicious BBQ Dinner served in a unique setting overlooking the African-inspired wildlife habitat. As you dine, be entertained by the spectacular Rhythm of Africa Fire Dance Show, featuring energetic performances, traditional music, fire displays, and vibrant cultural entertainment that perfectly completes your evening.

Before returning to your hotel, take part in a special Animal Encounter, where you'll have the chance to meet and learn more about some of the park's fascinating animals through an interactive educational presentation.

Whether you're travelling as a couple, with friends, or bringing the whole family, the Bali Night Safari Experience offers the perfect combination of wildlife, adventure, entertainment, and dining — creating magical memories long after the sun goes down.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is hotel transfer included?+

Yes. Complimentary return hotel transfers are included for guests staying in Kuta, Seminyak, Legian, Sanur, and Ubud.

Is dinner included?+

Yes. The package includes a delicious BBQ dinner served during your Night Safari experience.

Can I feed the animals?+

Yes. During the Night Safari Journey, you'll have the opportunity to hand-feed selected herbivorous animals under the supervision of experienced park staff.

Will I see lions and tigers?+

Yes. The Night Safari offers the opportunity to observe lions, tigers, and many other wildlife species roaming freely from the safety of the safari tram. However, animal activity may vary naturally.

Is this tour suitable for children?+

Absolutely. The Bali Night Safari is one of Bali's most family-friendly attractions and is suitable for guests of all ages.

What should I wear?+

We recommend comfortable clothing and walking shoes. Bringing a light jacket is advisable, as the evening can be cooler.

Are the animals kept safely away from visitors?+

Yes. The safari is conducted using specially designed secure trams that allow guests to safely observe wildlife at close range while ensuring the welfare of both visitors and animals.

Can I customize the itinerary?+

This experience follows the official Bali Safari Park Night Safari schedule. However, as transportation is private, hotel pick-up times can be adjusted within operational availability.
