About this tour

Escape to the heart of Bali with our Ubud River Rafting Adventure, a full-day private tour that combines exciting outdoor adventure, authentic Balinese coffee culture, and one of Ubud's most iconic wildlife attractions. From navigating the rapids of the Ayung River to tasting locally grown coffee and meeting playful monkeys in a lush tropical forest, this tour offers the perfect balance of excitement, relaxation, and cultural discovery.

Your adventure begins with an unforgettable Ayung River Rafting experience, one of Bali's most popular outdoor activities. Paddle through crystal-clear waters as you navigate exciting Class II and III rapids surrounded by lush rainforest, cascading waterfalls, towering cliffs, and beautifully carved stone reliefs along the riverbanks. Suitable for both beginners and experienced rafters, this adventure offers the perfect combination of adrenaline and breathtaking scenery.

After your rafting experience, enjoy a delicious lunch before continuing to a traditional Coffee & Tea Plantation nestled in the cool highlands of Ubud. Learn about Bali's famous coffee-making process, discover a variety of locally grown herbs and spices, and sample an assortment of freshly brewed coffees and herbal teas while overlooking beautiful tropical plantations. Guests also have the opportunity to learn about the world-famous Luwak Coffee and purchase locally produced products.

Complete your journey at the renowned Ubud Monkey Forest, a peaceful nature reserve and sacred sanctuary home to hundreds of free-roaming long-tailed macaques. Wander beneath ancient banyan trees, discover centuries-old temple ruins, and experience one of Bali's most beloved attractions while observing the monkeys in their natural habitat.

Whether you're seeking outdoor adventure, cultural experiences, or unforgettable moments surrounded by nature, the Ubud River Rafting Adventure promises an exciting day filled with discovery, relaxation, and lasting memories.

Good to Know

  • No previous rafting experience is required. Professional guides provide a full safety briefing before the activity.
  • Ayung River rafting is suitable for beginners, families, and adventurous travelers with a moderate level of fitness.
  • Participants should be comfortable walking down and up stairs to access the river.
  • Safety equipment, including helmets and life jackets, is provided and must be worn throughout the rafting activity.
  • Shower and changing room facilities are available after rafting.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous rafting experience?+

No. Ayung River rafting is suitable for beginners. Professional rafting guides will provide complete safety instructions before the trip begins.

Is the rafting safe?+

Yes. Professional guides, certified safety equipment, and international safety procedures are provided throughout the experience.

Are shower and changing facilities available?+

Yes. Clean shower rooms and changing facilities are available at the rafting base camp after your adventure.

Is coffee tasting included?+

Yes. A selection of locally produced Balinese coffee and herbal teas is included. Premium Luwak Coffee tasting may require an additional fee, depending on the plantation's policy.

Can I interact with the monkeys?+

You can observe the monkeys up close, but visitors are advised not to feed, touch, or disturb them. Please follow the staff's instructions for a safe and enjoyable visit.

Is this tour suitable for children?+

Yes. Children are welcome to join, provided they meet the rafting operator's minimum age requirements and are accompanied by an adult.

Are all entrance fees included?+

Yes. Rafting admission, Coffee & Tea Plantation entrance, Monkey Forest entrance, and all attraction fees are included in the all-inclusive package.

Can I customize the itinerary?+

Yes. As this is a private tour, minor itinerary adjustments can often be arranged based on your preferences and time availability.
