Nusa Penida

Ultimate East Nusa Penida Itinerary

MyBroBali Travel Guide · 5 min read

If you're looking for breathtaking ocean views, dramatic cliffs, and pristine white-sand beaches, East Nusa Penida is the perfect destination. Less crowded than the western side of the island, East Nusa Penida offers a peaceful escape with some of Bali's most spectacular coastal scenery.

This one-day itinerary takes you to three of the island's most iconic attractions: Diamond Beach, Atuh Beach, and Rumah Pohon (Molenteng Tree House).

Discover the Beauty of East Nusa Penida

Begin your adventure at Diamond Beach, famous for its towering limestone cliffs, crystal-clear turquoise waters, and powdery white sand. The panoramic viewpoint overlooking the beach is one of the most photographed locations in Nusa Penida, while the staircase leading down offers adventurous travellers the opportunity to experience the beach up close.

Next, visit the stunning Atuh Beach, a secluded paradise surrounded by dramatic rock formations and calm blue waters. Its peaceful atmosphere makes it an ideal place to relax, take in the breathtaking scenery, or simply enjoy the natural beauty of the island.

Complete your journey at the iconic Rumah Pohon (Molenteng Tree House), one of the most famous viewpoints in Nusa Penida. Perched on the edge of a cliff, the tree house offers spectacular views over Diamond Beach, Atuh Beach, and the surrounding coastline, making it a must-visit destination for photographers and nature lovers.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to explore East Nusa Penida is during the dry season (April to October) when sunny weather and clear skies provide the best conditions for sightseeing and photography. Taking one of the earliest fast boats from Bali is recommended to maximize your time on the island and avoid the midday crowds.

Travel Tips

Wear comfortable walking shoes, as several attractions involve climbing stairs and walking on uneven paths. Bring sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, drinking water, and a camera to capture the incredible scenery. If you plan to visit the beach, don't forget your swimsuit and a towel.

Why Visit East Nusa Penida?

East Nusa Penida is the perfect destination for travellers seeking dramatic landscapes, peaceful beaches, and unforgettable viewpoints. Compared to the western side of the island, it offers a quieter atmosphere while showcasing some of Indonesia's most spectacular coastal scenery.
