About this tour

Discover the breathtaking beauty of Nusa Penida, Bali's most spectacular neighbouring island, on this unforgettable full-day adventure. Famous for its dramatic coastal cliffs, crystal-clear waters, and world-renowned viewpoints, the Nusa Penida West Island Tour is the perfect getaway for travellers seeking stunning landscapes and unforgettable photo opportunities.

Your journey begins with a scenic fast boat ride from Sanur Harbor to Nusa Penida, where your local driver will be ready to take you around the island's most iconic attractions.

Your first stop is the legendary Kelingking Beach, one of Bali's most photographed destinations. Famous for its T-Rex-shaped cliff formation and breathtaking turquoise waters, this spectacular viewpoint offers panoramic views that have become an international travel icon.

Continue to the unique Broken Beach, a natural rock arch formed by centuries of ocean erosion. Surrounded by dramatic limestone cliffs and the deep blue sea, this geological wonder is one of the island's most remarkable natural attractions.

Just a short walk away lies Angel's Billabong, a stunning natural infinity pool carved into the coastal rocks. During calm tides, visitors can admire its crystal-clear water and incredible ocean scenery, making it another must-visit location on the island.

End your adventure at the beautiful Crystal Bay, a peaceful white-sand beach known for its calm waters, swaying palm trees, and spectacular ocean views. It's the perfect place to relax, swim, or simply unwind before returning to Bali.

Whether you're a photography enthusiast, nature lover, or first-time visitor to Bali, the Nusa Penida West Island Tour offers an unforgettable island experience filled with breathtaking scenery and unforgettable memories.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are the fast boat tickets included?+

Yes. Return fast boat tickets between Sanur and Nusa Penida are included in the all-inclusive package.

Can I swim at Crystal Bay?+

Yes. Swimming is possible depending on sea conditions. Please always follow the safety instructions provided by your guide.

Is this tour suitable for children?+

Yes. However, parents should closely supervise children, especially at cliff viewpoints and rocky coastal areas.

Will we hike down to Kelingking Beach?+

No. This tour includes the famous Kelingking viewpoint. Hiking down to the beach is optional and not recommended due to the steep and challenging trail within the tour's time frame.

What happens if the sea is rough?+

Boat departures are subject to weather and sea conditions. If conditions are deemed unsafe, operators may adjust departure times or reschedule the tour to ensure passenger safety.
