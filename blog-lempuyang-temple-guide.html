Complete Guide to Lempuyang Temple (Gate of Heaven)

MyBroBali Travel Guide · 7 min read

One of Bali's most iconic landmarks, Lempuyang Temple, is famous for its breathtaking Gate of Heaven, where visitors can capture the illusion of standing between heaven and earth with the majestic Mount Agung in the background. Located in the eastern part of Bali, this sacred temple is not only a world-famous photography destination but also one of the island's oldest and most important Hindu temples.

If you're planning to visit East Bali, this guide will help you make the most of your journey to the Gate of Heaven.

What is Lempuyang Temple?

Known locally as Pura Lempuyang Luhur, Lempuyang Temple is one of Bali's six holiest temples, believed to protect the island from spiritual imbalance. Sitting on the slopes of Mount Lempuyang, the temple complex consists of several temples connected by walking trails, with the famous Gate of Heaven located near the entrance of the first temple.

While many visitors come for the iconic photo, Lempuyang remains an active place of worship where Balinese Hindus regularly perform religious ceremonies. Visitors are encouraged to respect the local customs and maintain appropriate behavior throughout their visit.

Why is it Called the Gate of Heaven?

The famous split gate perfectly frames Mount Agung, Bali's highest and most sacred volcano. When photographed from the right angle, the gate creates the illusion of a doorway leading into the sky, making it one of the most recognizable images of Bali.

It's worth noting that the stunning reflection effect seen in many social media photos is not created by a natural lake or pool. Instead, local photographers create this effect using a mirror placed beneath the camera lens, producing the famous reflection that has become synonymous with Lempuyang Temple.

Explore More Than Just the Gate

Although the Gate of Heaven is the main attraction, East Bali offers several beautiful destinations that perfectly complement your visit.

Tirta Gangga Water Palace
Located about 20 minutes from Lempuyang Temple, Tirta Gangga is a former royal water palace known for its elegant fountains, koi ponds, stepping stones, and beautifully landscaped gardens. It's a peaceful place to stroll, relax, and capture stunning photographs.

Taman Ujung Water Palace
Continue your journey to Taman Ujung Water Palace, a historic royal complex featuring spacious gardens, picturesque bridges, and tranquil pools with views stretching toward both the mountains and the coastline. Its unique combination of Balinese and European architecture makes it one of East Bali's hidden treasures.

Together, these three attractions create one of the most popular full-day sightseeing routes in Bali.

Best Time to Visit

To enjoy the best experience at Lempuyang Temple, we highly recommend arriving before 6:00 AM.

The Gate of Heaven is one of Bali's most visited attractions, and photo queues often become very long shortly after sunrise. During weekends and peak holiday seasons, visitors may wait two to four hours or even longer for their turn to take the famous photo.

Arriving before 6:00 AM offers several advantages:

  • A much shorter waiting time for the Gate of Heaven photo.
  • A better chance of seeing Mount Agung clearly before clouds begin to form.
  • Cooler temperatures and a more comfortable atmosphere.
  • Softer morning light for beautiful photography.
  • A quieter and more peaceful temple experience.

For guests staying in Ubud, Seminyak, Kuta, Legian, Jimbaran, Nusa Dua, Sanur, or Canggu, hotel pick-up usually begins between 2:00 AM and 3:30 AM to ensure arrival before the crowds.

What You Should Know Before Visiting

Before planning your trip, here are a few important things to keep in mind:

  • The famous reflection photo is created using a mirror by local photographers—not by a natural body of water.
  • Photo queues can be very long, especially during the high season. Early arrival is highly recommended.
  • A sarong is required to enter the temple grounds and is usually provided at the entrance.
  • As Lempuyang Temple is an active place of worship, visitors should dress modestly, speak quietly, and respect religious ceremonies.
  • The weather can change quickly in the mountains, so the view of Mount Agung is never guaranteed. Clear mornings usually offer the best visibility.

What to Wear

Since Lempuyang Temple is a sacred religious site, visitors are expected to dress respectfully.

We recommend wearing:

  • Comfortable clothing
  • Light jacket for the early morning
  • Covered shoulders (recommended)
  • Long pants or a long skirt
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Hat and sunscreen for later in the day

A traditional sarong is required and is generally included with your entrance.

Suggested East Bali Itinerary

A well-planned full-day itinerary includes:

  • 02:30–03:30 AM – Hotel Pick-up
  • 05:30–06:00 AM – Arrive at Lempuyang Temple
  • 06:00–08:30 AM – Explore the temple and take photos at the Gate of Heaven
  • 09:00 AM – Visit Tirta Gangga Water Palace
  • 11:30 AM – Lunch at a local restaurant
  • 01:00 PM – Explore Taman Ujung Water Palace
  • 03:00 PM – Return to Hotel

This itinerary allows you to visit East Bali's most iconic attractions comfortably while avoiding the busiest crowds at Lempuyang Temple.

Who Is This Tour Suitable For?

This journey is ideal for:

  • First-time visitors to Bali
  • Couples and honeymooners
  • Families
  • Photography enthusiasts
  • Cultural travellers
  • Solo adventurers

The tour requires only light walking and is suitable for most fitness levels.

Final Thoughts

Lempuyang Temple is much more than a famous photo location—it's one of Bali's most sacred temples, offering breathtaking mountain views, rich cultural heritage, and a peaceful spiritual atmosphere. Combined with the elegant beauty of Tirta Gangga and the historic charm of Taman Ujung Water Palace, this journey showcases some of the very best that East Bali has to offer.

By arriving early and understanding what to expect before your visit, you'll enjoy a smoother, more rewarding experience while discovering one of Bali's most iconic destinations.
